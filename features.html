Whether you're capturing a quick thought or spending time with a page, Saya gives you a collection of ways to make your mark.

Choose from normal, calligraphy, taper and other available pen styles to create marks that suit your work.

02 · Draw & arrange

Think beyond the line.

Use drawing and selection tools to explore ideas visually, make adjustments and bring a page together.

Shapes & sketches

Draw freely and use shape tools to build diagrams, outlines and visual notes.

Lasso selection

Select content on the page, then move, resize or rotate it to refine your layout.

03 · Read & explore

Stay with the page.

Bring PDF documents into Saya and navigate through them in a reading-focused workspace.

PDF reading

Open documents and move between pages with a clear reading experience.

Reader Mode

Switch to a more focused view when you want the document to take centre stage.

04 · Your workspace

A little more order. A little less noise.

Use the app's workspace areas to return to recent notes and explore your content.

Customise available appearance and input settings to suit your preferred workflow.
