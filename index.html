04 / THE PERSON BEHIND IT

Made with curiosity.
Shaped with care.

Hi, I'm Sameer Hasan — the independent creator behind Saya. I started this project with a simple idea: make a thoughtful, comfortable space where people can write, draw, organise their notes and read PDFs without getting in the way of their ideas.

Saya keeps evolving through experimentation and small improvements. The goal stays simple: make it feel faster, smoother and more useful, while keeping the experience calm and personal.
