A little about us

Made with care.
Made to stay.

Saya began with a simple thought: the tools we use to collect our ideas should feel calm, personal and worth coming back to.

The person behind Saya

Sameer Hasan

I'm the creator of Saya. I wanted to build a thoughtful Android space for handwritten notes, drawing and reading — one that feels personal without getting in the way.

Saya is an independent project, shaped by curiosity, iteration and the belief that simple tools can make room for meaningful work.
